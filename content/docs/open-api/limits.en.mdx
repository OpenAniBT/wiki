Public RSS limit accepts a single decimal integer from 1 to 100 without leading zeros. Duplicate, empty, or malformed values return 400. Explicit limit=100 is normalized to a URL without the default parameter. Personal RSS has separate defaults and rules.

RSS q/search and Torznab q are limited to 160 UTF-8 bytes after normalization, and `/magnets` q to 300 characters. For matching rules, see [Release search](/en/docs/open-api#release-search). Bangumi search, seasonal title queries, and title matching have their own character limits. Public RSS has no cursor/page pagination; Torznab uses bounded offset.

For 429, honor Retry-After in seconds. A public RSS 503 means the backend is temporarily unavailable; it is not a rate limit. The application's 503 carries Retry-After: 30 and Cache-Control: no-store and is never replaced by an empty feed; wait, then retry. Do not assume every error includes Retry-After. Poll normal RSS at intervals of at least five minutes and avoid concurrent requests. Conditional requests returning 304 may still consume rate limits.
