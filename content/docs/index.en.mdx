AniBT is a BitTorrent distribution, indexing, and search platform designed for anime fans and fansub groups.

<Callout type="info">
  Browsing resources, copying magnet links, downloading `.torrent` files, and using public RSS feeds do not require an account. Registration is only needed if you want to follow anime, manage personal subscriptions, or publish releases as a fansub group member.
</Callout>

Whether you are a fansub team distributing releases, an anime fan looking for high-quality torrents, or a developer building ACG tools, you will find relevant guides here.

## Quick Navigation

<Tabs items={['Fansub Workflow', 'Anime Fans & Tracking', 'Developers & API']}>
  <Tab value="Fansub Workflow">
    Submit a release once on AniBT, and the system automatically dispatches it to major BT sites, with dedicated screenshot hosting and tracker communication.

    <Steps>
      <Step>
        ### Apply for Onboarding
        Register an account on AniBT, then submit your group details and release portfolio via email to obtain publisher permissions and a dedicated slug. See [Fansub Onboarding](/en/docs/apply).
      </Step>
      <Step>
        ### Configure Site Sync and Tracker
        Fill in credentials for connected BT platforms (Bangumi Moe, Mikan, DMHY, ACGNX, ACG.rip, Nyaa) in the group console. Include the [AniBT Tracker](/en/docs/tracker) when creating torrents. See [Site Sync](/en/docs/site-sync).
      </Step>
      <Step>
        ### Publish Releases
        Upload torrents directly via the web dashboard, or integrate your encoding pipelines using the [Release API](/en/docs/open-api/releases-publish). Supports pre-publish preview and dedicated image hosting.

        If anime search reports a temporary failure while publishing, retry later; this does not mean the anime is missing. If you know its BGM ID, you can bind the anime directly by ID.

        Normal edits change metadata for anime and other releases; they cannot add, replace or remove the original torrent. If a torrent exists, its magnet link must keep the same v1 `infohash`. You may change tracker, display-name and other parameters for that same hash. A magnet-only release can still change its magnet link, but normal editing cannot attach a torrent.
      </Step>
    </Steps>
  </Tab>

  <Tab value="Anime Fans & Tracking">
    Enjoy seamless anime searching and automated episode tracking without registration hurdles.

    <Steps>
      <Step>
        ### Search and Download
        Search anime titles by Chinese, Japanese, Bangumi subject ID, or fansub group tags. Each release detail page offers magnet links and standard `.torrent` downloads.

        Every `/magnets` keyword must appear in the release title, spec, or a title or alias of its anime, with no typo tolerance or prefix completion. Latin letters and digits must be whole words (`1080` doesn't find `1080p`); Chinese and Japanese match as contiguous characters (`鬼灭` finds releases of 鬼灭之刃). Results list newest first by default, and `-word` excludes releases you don't want. Anime search ranks by how closely a title matches, tolerates small spelling differences in long titles, and treats hiragana and katakana, and Traditional and Simplified characters, as the same. See the [Open API overview](/en/docs/open-api#release-search) for the rules.

        Release detail pages, `/magnets`, anime pages, and the release panel that opens when you click an anime poster fetch download addresses only when you click a button:

        - **Copy magnet link** and **Open magnet link** fetch the magnet link on click. If it cannot be fetched, you see "Download URL is unavailable". If the browser refuses to write to the clipboard, click **Copy again** in the notice. If the browser cannot open the magnet link directly, click **Open again** in the notice.
        - **Download torrent** downloads the `.torrent` file directly. On anime pages and in the release panel, the torrent button also downloads directly instead of opening the release detail page.
        - File list: on a release detail page, click **View file list** to load it. The file count appears after it loads.

        The **Releases** tab on an anime page has its own URL, `/anime/<id>?tab=releases`; the default **Release progress** tab is `/anime/<id>`. On `/magnets` and on a fansub group's **Releases** tab, previous and next are links that carry a `cursor` parameter, so you can open them in a new tab or share them. The group **Releases** tab also keeps its season filter in the URL (`season` and `year`, or `all`) and shows 20 releases per page. An expired page link returns to the first page for the same filters.
      </Step>
      <Step>
        ### Track via RSS
        Copy the anime-specific [RSS Feed URL](/en/docs/open-api/rss-anime) into your downloader (e.g., qBittorrent, Sonarr, FlexGet). New episodes will download automatically upon release.
      </Step>
      <Step>
        ### Manage Personal Subscriptions (Optional)
        Sign in and click "Subscribe" on an anime page. Filter by fansub group, subtitle language, resolution, file format, subtitle type, and tags, then click "Subscribe" to save. Leave a filter set to "All" to accept every value.

        To edit a subscription, open "Subscription" on the anime page or its card in "My Subscriptions". Your saved filters are filled in automatically and can be edited while more options load. If loading fails, retry without losing your edits. Click "Save" to apply changes or "Cancel" to discard them. Multiple selections within one filter match any selected value; different filters must all match.

        Copy the complete [personal subscription RSS](/en/docs/open-api/rss-subscriptions) link from "My Subscriptions" into your download tool to receive matching releases. Keep this link private: it contains an access credential. The public RSS link on an anime page does not use your personal filters.
      </Step>
    </Steps>
  </Tab>

  <Tab value="Developers & API">

    <Steps>

      <Step>
        ### Leverage HTTP 304 Caching
        All public feeds support weak ETags and `If-None-Match`. Include the previous ETag in polling loops to receive `304 Not Modified` when content is unchanged, saving bandwidth. See [HTTP Caching](/en/docs/open-api/caching).
      </Step>
      <Step>
        ### Automate Fansub Releases
        Developers with a Subtitle Group API Key can automate torrent uploads, metadata matching, external sync inspection, and release withdrawals via REST.
      </Step>
      <Step>

        The main site's `robots.txt`, sitemap shards, which pages are indexed, how the three language URLs point to each other (hreflang), and IndexNow notifications are described in [LLM & Agent Integration](/en/docs/llm).
      </Step>
    </Steps>
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

## Release Title Templates

Title templates derive codec, bit depth, audio and source fields from the technical tags in the publishing form. Custom and built-in templates use the same spelling rules. Each template keeps its bracket, parenthesis and subtitle-label style.

```text title="Put codec and bit depth in one pair of brackets"
[{{codecBitDepth}}]
```

For example, `H.264` with `8 bit` produces `[AVC 8bit]`, and `HEVC-10bit` produces `[HEVC 10bit]`. A single space separates codec and bit depth. If only one field is present, only that field is shown; if both are missing, the empty brackets are removed. Use `codec` and `bitDepth` separately to control their layout.

<TypeTable type={{
  "codec": { type: "string", description: "The normalized video codec name, such as AVC, HEVC, AV1, VP9, MPEG4, VC-1 or ProRes." },
  "bitDepth": { type: "string", description: "Recognized bit depth, formatted as 8bit or 10bit. Missing bit depth is not inferred from the codec." },
  "audioCodec": { type: "string", description: "Consistent audio names, such as AAC, OPUS, TrueHD or DTS-HD MA. Channel information in the tag is retained." },
  "source": { type: "string", description: "Consistent source names, such as WebRip, WEB-DL, BDRip or DVDRip. Hyphens within names are retained." },
  "dimensions": { type: "string", description: "Standard dimensions for the selected resolution, matching the dimensions formatter on resolution. This is not a measurement of the video file. 480p maps to 854x480; 360p maps to 640x360." }
}} />

Formatting applies when a template generates a title. It does not rewrite saved technical tags or bulk-update published titles or original torrent filenames. Templates that include extra tags retain unrecognized custom labels.

A tag is split into fields only when every remaining part is a separator or a known annotation (HDR10+, HDR/HDR10, DV/Dolby Vision, HLG, Main10/Hi10P/Ma10p, DualAudio, Atmos, or PGS/SRT/ASS/SUP/IDX). Compound tags that still contain unknown text, such as `DVD-BOX`, `CR版`, `TV版`, and `TV Size`, stay intact as one tag.

Repeated annotations or custom tags are emitted once. When both `HDR10+` and `HEVC HDR10+` are present, the title contains `[HDR10+]` only once.

Platform names use the same vocabulary as autofill. `TVer`, `U-NEXT`, `d-anime`, and `Lemino` are placed in the platform position of the classic brackets and `techParens`.

In the classic brackets, `AAC`, `AAC 2.0`, `AAC-LC`, and `HE-AAC` combine with AVC as `[AAC AVC]`. With no video codec, the output is `[AAC]`. `HE-AACv2` does not join this combination.

## Anime Metadata Updates

Anime metadata updates automatically from its sources. If an update is delayed, you do not need to keep refreshing the page. Waiting for a retry preserves the metadata already available.

<TypeTable type={{
  "Temporary failures": {
    "type": "Behavior",
    "description": "A confirmed timeout, connection failure or HTTP 500/502/503 response from a Bangumi metadata query waits 1 minute, 5 minutes, 15 minutes, then 1 hour for the first four consecutive failures. Later backoff is 12, 24, 48 and 96 hours, capped at 7 days. Up to 20% extra delay may be added within that cap. A later deadline required by the source still applies."
  },
  "Source limits or pauses": {
    "type": "Behavior",
    "description": "Rate limiting follows the waiting time supplied by the source, or 15 minutes when none is supplied. A source authentication failure pauses synchronization until an administrator checks and resumes it."
  },
  "Unconfirmed outcomes or repeated failures": {
    "type": "Behavior",
    "description": "An unconfirmed request outcome retains at least 12 hours of protection from that attempt. After five actual failures in one round, the next automatic round waits at least another 12 hours. Waiting itself does not count as a failure. Manual refresh cannot bypass source limits or protection for unconfirmed outcomes."
  },
  "Waiting status": {
    "type": "Behavior",
    "description": "The admin console shows the actual error time separately from the current waiting reason, earliest next query time and most recent accepted metadata. A paused source has no confirmed recovery time. The earliest retry time is not a completion promise."
  }
}} />

These rules cover anime metadata synchronization. For publishing releases to external sites, see [Site Sync](/en/docs/site-sync).

## Interface Languages

The AniBT main site has three interface languages: Simplified Chinese, Traditional Chinese, and English. The URL alone decides the page language, and the three URLs correspond one to one.

<TypeTable
  type={{
    '简体中文 (Simplified Chinese)': {
      type: 'No prefix',
      description: 'Example: https://anibt.net/anime/617123',
    },
    '繁體中文 (Traditional Chinese)': {
      type: '/zh-hant/',
      description: 'Example: https://anibt.net/zh-hant/anime/617123',
    },
    'English': {
      type: '/en/',
      description: 'Example: https://anibt.net/en/anime/617123',
    },
  }}
/>

- Every page has all three URLs, including public pages, the sign-in and sign-up pages, personal pages, and the fansub group console. Signing in from a Traditional Chinese or English page brings you back to a page in the same language.
- The language menu links to the same page in each language and remembers your choice. Your manual choice takes priority over your browser languages.
- The first time you open the homepage `/` directly in a tab, the site uses your remembered choice, then the first supported language in your browser's language list: English opens `/en`, and Traditional Chinese opens `/zh-hant`. Simplified Chinese, or no supported match, stays at `/`. `en-US` and `en-GB` match English; `zh-TW`, `zh-HK`, and `zh-MO` match Traditional Chinese.
- Automatic detection runs at most once per tab. Opening `/` directly again in the same tab keeps the Simplified Chinese homepage. Following a link back to the homepage from a detail page does not trigger detection. URLs with a language prefix and other pages keep the language specified by their URL.
- Automatic navigation requires scripts and session storage to be available in the browser. The Simplified Chinese homepage may appear briefly before navigation. Recognized crawlers and automation tools do not participate. You can use the language menu at any time.
- When no automatic navigation occurs, a reader who previously chose Traditional Chinese or English sees a one-time language hint on a page without a prefix. Select "Switch" or 「切換」 to open that page in the offered language; otherwise the current page stays open.
- Main-site language prefixes are lowercase only: `/zh-Hant/…` and `/EN/…` return 404. This wiki's own Traditional Chinese URLs, `/zh-Hant/docs/…`, follow a different rule.
- Interface text follows the language. Release titles, release notes, and fansub group names stay in their original language in all three.
- Traditional Chinese pages show each anime by its official Traditional Chinese title. An anime without one gets a title converted automatically from its Simplified Chinese name, and synopses are always converted. Simplified Chinese and English pages show the Simplified Chinese title and synopsis.
- APIs (`/api/…`), RSS, Torznab, sitemaps, `robots.txt`, release preview links, and the sign-in callback and OAuth authorization URLs have a single URL with no language prefix.
