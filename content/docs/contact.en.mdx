AniBT is maintained by anime enthusiasts and open-source contributors. If you have questions, need to request permissions, or need to handle copyright matters, reach out to our official support team.

<Callout type="info">
  **Official Support Email**: [support@anibt.net](mailto:support@anibt.net)
</Callout>

## Inquiries & Email Guidelines

To help us assist you as efficiently as possible, please follow these guidelines when emailing us:

<Tabs items={['Fansub Onboarding & Whitelisting', 'Technical Issues & API Troubleshooting', 'Copyright & Takedown (DMCA)', 'Feedback & Suggestions']}>
  <Tab value="Fansub Onboarding & Whitelisting">
    - **Subject**: `Fansub Group Onboarding - <Group Name>` or `Nyaa Proxy Whitelist - <Group Name>`
    - **Sender Address**: Please send from the email address registered on AniBT.
    - **Required Info**: Group name, permanent slug, profile, and release history links (e.g., DMHY, Mikan). See the [Onboarding Guide](/en/docs/apply).
  </Tab>

  <Tab value="Technical Issues & API Troubleshooting">
    - **Subject**: `[Bug Report] <Affected Page or Endpoint>`
    - **Required Info**:
      1. Specific URL or API endpoint
      2. The curl command or request parameters used (mask any private API keys)
      3. The returned error JSON, especially the `requestId` field; for an error on the website, copy the “error code · request ID” line at the end of the message, or what “Copy all” on the error page gives you
      4. Exact timestamp of the incident
  </Tab>

  <Tab value="Copyright & Takedown (DMCA)">
    - **Subject**: `DMCA Takedown Notice - <Release Name>`
    - **Required Info**: Proof of copyright ownership or authorization, the exact AniBT release detail page URL, and the legal representative's contact information. We will verify and take appropriate action promptly.
  </Tab>

  <Tab value="Feedback & Suggestions">
    - **Subject**: `[Suggestion] <Summary of Your Idea>`
    - **Required Info**: We welcome your ideas regarding browsing experience, RSS filtering, multi-site sync, and new features. For improvements to open-source ecosystem tools, feel free to open an issue on the relevant GitHub repository.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

## Response Times

- **Standard Inquiries**: Typically reviewed and answered within **1 to 3 business days**.
- **Copyright Takedown**: Given complete documentation, prioritized within **24 hours** on business days.
- **Queue Note**: If you haven't received a response after 7 days, please check your spam folder before sending a follow-up email.
